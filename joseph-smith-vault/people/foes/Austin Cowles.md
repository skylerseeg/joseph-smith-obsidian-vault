# 👤 Austin Cowles

**Tags:** #person/foe #era/nauvoo
**Born:** ~1795
**Died:** Unknown
**Occupation:** Farmer, Nauvoo High Council member
**Excommunicated:** 1844

---

## Summary

Austin Cowles was a member of the Nauvoo High Council — one of the governing bodies of the church — who broke with Smith in 1844 over plural marriage and theocratic governance. His position on the High Council gave his defection institutional weight: he was not a peripheral critic but a sitting church leader. He became one of the seven publishers of the *Nauvoo Expositor* and provided sworn affidavits about what he had witnessed inside church governance.

---

## High Council Position

The Nauvoo High Council was the local ecclesiastical court and governing body, positioned just below the First Presidency and Quorum of the Twelve. Cowles had held this position for several years, giving him direct knowledge of church governance decisions — including discussions of plural marriage — that were denied to ordinary members.

---

## Break with Smith

Cowles's break came in early 1844 when [[William Law]] organized the opposition that would become the Reformed Church. Cowles joined this faction and was excommunicated along with the Law group. His specific grievances, as stated in his *Expositor* affidavit, were:
- Smith had taught plural marriage as a private doctrine while publicly denying it
- The [[Council of Fifty]] represented a dangerous fusion of church and political power
- Smith was moving toward theocratic kingship

---

## The *Nauvoo Expositor*

Cowles provided one of the most significant affidavits printed in the [[Nauvoo Expositor]], attesting to Smith's private teachings on plural marriage and the Council of Fifty as a sitting High Council member. His insider status made his testimony harder to dismiss than that of more peripheral figures.

---

## After the Martyrdom

Cowles gave further testimony after Smith's death regarding what he had witnessed inside Nauvoo governance. He did not rejoin the LDS church.
