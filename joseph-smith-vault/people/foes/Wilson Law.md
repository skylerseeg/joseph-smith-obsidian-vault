# 👤 Wilson Law

**Tags:** #person/foe #era/nauvoo
**Occupation:** Merchant, Major General, Nauvoo Legion
**Excommunicated:** April 1844
**Relation:** Brother of [[William Law]]

---

## Summary

Wilson Law was the brother of [[William Law]] and jointly led the opposition that produced the *Nauvoo Expositor*. As a Major General in the Nauvoo Legion — the church's own military force — his defection carried particular institutional weight: Smith lost not just a wealthy merchant but a senior military commander.

---

## Role in Nauvoo

Wilson Law was a prosperous Canadian merchant who arrived in Nauvoo alongside his brother William. He held two significant positions simultaneously:
- **Major General, Nauvoo Legion** — senior military command in Smith's personal army
- **Merchant** — one of the wealthier men in Nauvoo

The combination of military rank and commercial resources made Wilson Law a significant figure in both the church's power structure and its economic life.

---

## The Break

Like his brother, Wilson Law's break with Smith came over plural marriage. He participated in organizing the **Reformed Mormon Church** (also called the True Church of Jesus Christ) in April 1844 — a competing organization that claimed to represent original Mormon doctrine without polygamy or theocratic governance.

Both Law brothers were **excommunicated in April 1844** — the same month the Reformed church was organized.

---

## The *Nauvoo Expositor*

Wilson Law was among the publishers of the *Nauvoo Expositor* — the June 7, 1844 dissident newspaper that triggered Smith's final crisis. His role was primarily organizational and financial rather than editorial.

---

## After the Martyrdom

Wilson Law left Nauvoo after Smith's death. Unlike his brother William, he left fewer documentary traces of his post-Nauvoo life. He did not rejoin the LDS church or its offshoots.
