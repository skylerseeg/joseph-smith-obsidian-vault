# ⚖️ Justin Butterfield

**Tags:** #person/ally #role/lawyer #era/nauvoo
**Born:** 1790, Keene, New Hampshire
**Died:** October 23, 1855, Chicago, Illinois
**Occupation:** Lawyer, U.S. Attorney for Illinois

---

## Summary

Justin Butterfield was the U.S. Attorney for the District of Illinois who delivered Joseph Smith's most significant legal victory — the defeat of Missouri's 1843 extradition attempt before federal judge [[Nathaniel Pope]] in Springfield.

---

## The 1843 Boggs Extradition Hearing

**Background:** Missouri Governor [[Thomas Reynolds]] sought to extradite Smith on the charge of being an accessory to the attempted assassination of former Governor [[Lilburn W. Boggs]], shot in May 1842. The alleged shooter was Orrin Porter Rockwell; Smith was accused of conspiracy.

**The hearing:** Held before U.S. District Judge [[Nathaniel Pope]] in Springfield, Illinois, January 1843.

**Butterfield's argument:** Missouri could not legally extradite Joseph Smith for a crime allegedly committed while Smith was **in Illinois, not Missouri**. The extradition statutes required the accused to be a "fugitive from justice" — meaning someone who was physically present in the demanding state when the crime occurred and then fled.

Smith had not been in Missouri at the time of the Boggs shooting.

**Outcome:** Judge Pope agreed. Smith was freed.

---

## Significance

The Butterfield victory is the clearest example of competent legal counsel converting Smith's legal vulnerability into a constitutional argument about the limits of extradition law. It bought Smith approximately 18 more months of life.

Smith reportedly described Butterfield's performance in court as the finest legal argument he had ever heard.
