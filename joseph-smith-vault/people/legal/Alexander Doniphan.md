# ⚖️ Alexander Doniphan

**Tags:** #person/ally #role/lawyer #era/missouri
**Full Name:** Alexander William Doniphan
**Occupation:** General, Missouri Militia; Lawyer
**Region:** Missouri

---

## Summary

Alexander Doniphan is one of the most heroic figures in Joseph Smith's legal history — a non-Mormon military officer and lawyer who risked his career by refusing a direct order to execute Smith and his companions after their surrender at Far West, Missouri.

---

## The Refusal to Execute (1838)

After the surrender of Far West in October 1838, General Samuel D. Lucas issued an order for Doniphan to execute Joseph Smith and other Mormon leaders by firing squad the following morning.

Doniphan's response is one of the most dramatic moments in the entire Smith narrative:

> He refused the order, reportedly telling Lucas that it was "cold-blooded murder" and that if he carried it out, he would hold Lucas personally responsible before God and man.

This single act of defiance saved Smith's life and gave him the opportunity to eventually escape to Illinois.

---

## Legal Defense in Missouri

Beyond the execution refusal, Doniphan served as Smith's lead legal counsel during the 1838 Missouri military proceedings, navigating one of the most hostile legal environments Smith ever faced.

---

## Character Assessment

Doniphan represents a rare figure in Smith's story: a powerful non-Mormon who applied the law consistently regardless of the popular sentiment against the Saints. His trajectory is the inverse of [[Stephen A. Douglas]] — he did not abandon Smith when it became politically costly.

---

## Later Career

Doniphan went on to command the famous "Doniphan's Expedition" during the Mexican-American War (1846–1847), one of the longest military marches in American history, cementing his reputation as one of Missouri's most celebrated military figures.
