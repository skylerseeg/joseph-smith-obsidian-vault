## The Kirtland Land Bubble — Data

| Metric | 1830 | 1836 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Church membership | ~1,000 | ~3,000 | +200% |
| Land price per acre | $7 | $44 | +529% |
| Kirtland Safety Society charter | — | Denied | Political opposition |

This table captures the core economic dynamic: explosive growth created paper wealth, the Safety Society was an attempt to monetize that wealth, the charter denial turned the attempt illegal, and the collapse destroyed the wealth and the loyalty network simultaneously.

## The Apostasy Wave — Anatomy

The Kirtland Safety Society failure produced the **first mass defection** from Smith's senior leadership:

**Pre-1837:** All 12 Apostles loyal
**Post-1837:** At minimum 6 of 12 either excommunicated or disaffected within two years

The pattern in each case:
1. Man invested personal savings in Safety Society
2. Notes became worthless; savings lost
3. Man concluded Smith's prophetic claims were inconsistent with the economic disaster
4. Formal accusation → excommunication OR departure

**The scribal dimension:** [[Warren Parrish]] as bank teller had detailed knowledge of the Society's financial operations — knowledge he used to credibly accuse Smith of deliberate fraud rather than mere mismanagement.
