# 👤 Joseph Knight Sr.

**Tags:** #person/ally #era/new-york #era/ohio
**Born:** November 3, 1772, Oakham, Massachusetts
**Died:** February 2, 1847, Mount Pisgah, Iowa
**Occupation:** Farmer, Mill Owner

---

## Summary

Joseph Knight Sr. was one of the most practically important figures in Joseph Smith's early career — a steady, generous patron who provided essential material support during the Book of Mormon translation when the Smith family had almost nothing.

---

## The Translation Support

Knight and his family owned a mill and farm in Colesville, New York, near the Harmony, Pennsylvania area where Smith conducted much of the translation. He provided:
- **Paper** for recording the translation (a significant expense in 1828–1829)
- **Food** — on multiple occasions he loaded a wagon with provisions and delivered them to Smith
- **Horses** and transportation
- Moral support and protection during a period of intense local hostility

Smith later wrote that without Knight's assistance, the translation could not have proceeded.

---

## Conversion

Knight and his family were among the earliest converts, baptized in June 1830. His son **Newel Knight** became a significant early church leader and presided over the Colesville Branch — the first organized branch of the church.

---

## The "Knight Network"

The extended Knight family represented a critical early support infrastructure:
- Joseph Knight Sr. — financial and material patron
- **Newel Knight** — branch president, close friend, subject of one of Smith's first recorded miracle accounts (casting out a devil)
- Joseph Knight Jr. — also an early convert

---

## Later Life

Knight remained loyal through Ohio and Missouri, suffering the dispossessions and expulsions alongside the main body of Saints. He died in Iowa in 1847 while en route to Utah — too elderly to complete the journey.
