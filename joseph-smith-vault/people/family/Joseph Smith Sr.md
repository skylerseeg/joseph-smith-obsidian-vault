# 👤 Joseph Smith Sr.

**Tags:** #person/ally #role/family #era/new-york #era/ohio
**Born:** July 12, 1771, Topsfield, Massachusetts
**Died:** September 14, 1840, Nauvoo, Illinois
**Occupation:** Farmer, Cooper, Storekeeper
**Relation:** Father of Joseph Smith Jr.

---

## Summary

Joseph Smith Sr. was the patriarch of the Smith family and one of his son's earliest and most loyal supporters. His life was shaped by poverty, frequent relocation, and a deep engagement with the folk religious traditions of early New England — all of which directly shaped the environment in which Joseph Jr. developed his prophetic claims.

---

## Background

- Born in Massachusetts into a family with a tradition of visionary experiences — his own mother, Mary Duty Smith, reportedly had prophetic dreams
- Married Lucy Mack in 1796
- The family suffered repeated financial setbacks, including a failed ginseng trade and land disputes in Vermont
- Relocated the family to Palmyra, New York, in **1816** following crop failures

---

## Role in the Prophetic Network

Joseph Sr. was embedded in the **folk magic / treasure-seeking subculture** of the Burned-over District. He was not an embarrassment to his son's claims — he was their cultural precondition. Both father and son participated in treasure-seeking activities that were common in rural upstate New York.

- Supported Joseph Jr.'s early claims about the golden plates without public hesitation
- Was among the first to accept baptism when the church was organized in 1830
- Served as the first **Patriarch of the Church** — a role of significant spiritual authority involving the giving of individual blessings

---

## Death

Joseph Sr. died in Nauvoo, Illinois, on September 14, 1840, at age 69 — before the most turbulent final years of his son's life. His death was reportedly a peaceful one, surrounded by family. He blessed each of his children on his deathbed.
