## 📅 Life Phases at a Glance

- **1805–1823** — Birth, Palmyra, NY. Folk magic world. Death of Alvin.
- **1823–1831** — First Vision, plates, translation. Book of Mormon published. Church organized.
- **1831–1838** — Ohio (Kirtland). Temple. Banking crisis. Network fractures.
- **1838–1839** — Missouri War. Extermination Order. Liberty Jail.
- **1839–1844** — Nauvoo. Zenith of power. Presidential campaign. Martyrdom.

---

## 🔑 Core Tension

> Joseph Smith's life was a collision between **prophetic authority** and **frontier legal/media systems** that were not designed to accommodate a cohesive, armed, theocratic minority.

The two foundational papers in this vault analyze this collision from two angles:

1. **[[The Prophetic Circle]]** — Socio-political and economic mapping of his network.
2. **[[The Media the Law and the Prophet]]** — Socio-legal analysis of press and judiciary.
