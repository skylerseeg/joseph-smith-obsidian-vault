In JS’s discourse on the morning of 11 June, he commented on the relationship between the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost and critiqued the traditional Christian understanding of the Trinity; this may have led to comments on divine corporeality and omnipresence. On 9 July, JS answered questions posed by an unnamed—and possibly fictional—interlocutor in a fashion similar to that in the undated discourse recorded by Burgess.
