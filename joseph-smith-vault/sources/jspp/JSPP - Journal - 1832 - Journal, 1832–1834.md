I feel to be grateful to my heavenly father for all his goodness and mercy to me and family. I have been in much tribulation and affliction, but the Lord has been my support and strong tower... ---

## Source Note

Joseph Smith began this journal in November 1832 at Kirtland, Ohio. It is the earliest surviving journal kept by Smith himself, making it a primary source of singular historical importance. The journal covers the period from 27 November 1832 to 5 December 1834 and is held at the Church History Library in Salt Lake City, Utah.
