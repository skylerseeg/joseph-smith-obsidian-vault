Davis, who was also listed as a witness in the trial record, although there is no indication that he testified. Both Solomon Denton and Marvel Davis had connections to the church, but Denton had been excommunicated two or three months before the June trial and Davis was disaffected at the time of the trial. (“To Sidney Rigdon—Letter No. 2,” Painesville [OH] Telegraph, 26 May 1837, [3]; Grandison Newell, Letter to the Editor, Painesville Telegraph, 30 June 1837, [3]; Transcript of Proceedings, 5 June 1837, State of Ohio on Complaint of Newell v.
