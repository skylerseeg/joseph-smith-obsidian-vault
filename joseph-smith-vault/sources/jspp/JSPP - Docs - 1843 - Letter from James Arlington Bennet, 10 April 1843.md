According to Bennett, Rockwell left Nauvoo soon after, and when Bennett asked JS where Rockwell had gone, JS reportedly replied that Rockwell had gone to fulfill the prophecy regarding Boggs’s violent death. Bennett claimed that Rockwell later admitted to him that he was in Boggs’s neighborhood at the time of the shooting but that his accusers would have to prove he was the attempted assassin.
