## Full Transcript

NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info Jany 9, 1841 <​$350​> Received of Joseph Smith the sum of Three hundred and fifty dollars, being the amount of money which I loaned to Mr Peter Hawes. Signature of Samuel Cope.Samuel Cope [p. [1]]

  Endorsement in handwriting of Robert B. Thompson.SamlCope rct
