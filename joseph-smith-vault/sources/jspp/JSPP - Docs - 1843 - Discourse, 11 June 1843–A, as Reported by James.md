## Full Transcript

John the revelator says that the testimoney of Jesus is the Spirit of prophicy. Now if any man has the testimoney of Jesus has he not the spirit of prophicy and if he has the spirit of prophicy I ask is he not a prophet and if a prophet will he not receive revalation And any man that [p. [9]]

  does not receive revelation for himself must be damned for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophicy for Christ says ask and you shall receive.1See Matthew 21:22; Book of Mormon, 1840 ed., 142 [Enos 1:15]; and Revelation, Apr. 1829–A [D&C 6:5]. And if he happens to receive anything I ask will it not be a revelation. And if any man has not the testimony of Jesus or the spirit of God he is none of his <​namely​> Christ’s And if not his he must be damn’d. What are we to understand by a Prophet It is his character to predict things that are in the future. I ask what right has any man or set of men or preist or set of preists to say if a man will not do so and so he shall be damn’d. Is he not takeing upon himself or assumeing the character of a Prophet consequently he must either be a true or false Prophet. [p. [10]]
