Although Brown was not a member of the church, he was friends with JS. In January 1843, JS visited Brown’s home in Rushville, Illinois. Although the reliability of Jackson’s accusation is difficult to assess, Utah-era Council of Fifty minutes confirm that JS connected Brown’s weapon with the Russian mission. In 1851, the Council of Fifty discussed Brown’s offer to sell the weapon to Brigham Young to be used to defend Latter-day Saint settlements in the Great Basin.
