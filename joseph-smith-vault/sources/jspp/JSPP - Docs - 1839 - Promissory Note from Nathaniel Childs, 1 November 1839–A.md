## Full Transcript

NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info Novr. 1st. 1841 [1839] $200.— On or before the first day of November One thousand eight hundred and forty two I promise to pay Mr Joseph Smith or order the sum of Two hundred dollars with interest for value received Signature of Nathaniel Childs.Nathaniel ChildsView Full Bio [p.
