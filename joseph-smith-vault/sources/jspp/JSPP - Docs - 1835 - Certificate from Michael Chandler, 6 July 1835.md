While it is possible that some non-Mormons were involved in the purchase, all other reports refute the notion that they bought the items “united together.” Today, only fragments remain of the papyri JS acquired from Chandler. After being acquired by the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York City as early as 1918, they were given to The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in 1967 and are now housed in the Church History Library in Salt Lake City.
