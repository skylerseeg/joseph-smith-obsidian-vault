## Full Transcript

AnnanTown located about fifteen miles southeast of Dumfries, Scotland. Situated on east bank of River Annan two miles north of mouth of river on Solway Firth of Irish Sea. Played important role in Wars of Succession and was home to castle of Robert the Bruce. ...More Info, Scotland Septr 1842— All Glory) to God) Sir, It Having come to my knowledge that you are disgracing the Christian Religion, likewise treating your indulgent Maker with contempt by publishing a pretended work entitled the book of Mormon;1Cadett may have come in contact with the edition of the Book of Mormon published in Liverpool in 1841. now I as the Shepherd (who was in truth foretold to come by the prophet Mahomet [p. [1]]

  as a Guide to Mankind)2See Koran 42:13. demand that you immediately confess publicly your error and cause the said attempt at divine inspiration to be withdrawn instantly, but should you persist after this warning at endeavouring to dupe the Public, recollect I have now done my Duty and clear myself off you, when at the awful day of judgement all [p. [2]]

  shall appear before the tribunal of Almighty God the only Creator and preserver of Heaven and Earth— Hoping this may sink deeply into your heart and work your salvation I subscribe myself very respectfully Yours William Cadettca. 1810–6 Feb. 1891.
