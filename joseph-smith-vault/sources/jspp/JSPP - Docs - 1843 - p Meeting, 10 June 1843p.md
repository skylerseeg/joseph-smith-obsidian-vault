## Full Transcript

City of NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info. June 3d.1843 To the Hon City Councel gentleman we your undersignd Petitioners having understood that thare is a Hog Law Passed in the CityPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info, we your humble Petitioners Pray your Hon Body to Repeal the above Law. and to pass some law to Make the Owners of unruly Hogs Acountible for all Damages Committed by them. Gentleme we do Belive that you will Act for the Best for the Peace and Comfort of the Citizens of NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri.
