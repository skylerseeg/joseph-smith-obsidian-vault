The <​Select​> Committee to whom was referred the Bill relative to Writs of Habeas Corpus“Have the body”; a written order from a court of competent jurisdiction commanding anyone having a person in custody to produce such person at a certain time and place and to state the reasons why he or she is being held in custody.
