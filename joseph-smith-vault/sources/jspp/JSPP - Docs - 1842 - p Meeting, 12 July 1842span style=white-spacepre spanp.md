## Full Transcript

Insertion in handwriting of James Sloan.<​Sec. 1.—​> Be it ordained by the city council of the city of NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info that all laws & ordinances passed by the city council untill the laws are printed be inserted in the book that is now being printed.— <​all ordinances to the contrary notwithstanding​> Signature in handwriting of James Sloan.Joseph Smith, Mayor. Endorsements in handwriting of James Sloan.<​rules disprd with, & passed​> <​passed July 12[th] 1842.​> <​James Sloan28 Oct. 1792–24 Oct. 1886. City recorder, notary public, attorney, judge, farmer. Born in Donaghmore, Co. Tyrone, Ireland. Son of Alexander Sloan and Anne. Married Mary Magill. Baptized into Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Ordained an elder, ...View Full Bio, Recorder.— An ordinance authorizing the publication of additional Laws and Ordinances.—​> [p. [1]]
