## Footnotes

**[1]** The Saints nominated a full ticket of candidates in May, and JS called for independent candidates in July. Nevertheless, church members joined together in support of the Democratic ticket in the election in August. (Minutes, 26 May 1842; Letter to the Citizens of Hancock County, ca. 2 July 1842.)
**[2]** The election took place on Monday, 1 August 1842. (“The Election,” Warsaw [IL] Signal, 6 Aug. 1842, [2].)
**[3]** Along with their publication in local newspapers, party tickets were sometimes published in circulars and broadsides. (See, for example, Election Ticket, 1846, in Chicago Historical Society, Collection of Manuscripts about Mormons, 1832–1954, microfilm, CHL.)
