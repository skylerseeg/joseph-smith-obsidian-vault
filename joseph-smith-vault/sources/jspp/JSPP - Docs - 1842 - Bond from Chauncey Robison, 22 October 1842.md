Robison bound himself to deed the land to JS if he made the scheduled payments. Robison’s bond contained more detail than was commonly included in similar land purchase agreements. For example, Robison amended the text of the bond to reflect minor details in the promissory notes, such as canceling Clayton’s first name and replacing it with the abbreviation of “Wm.” found on the notes. This change ensured that there could be no question about the legality of the bond or notes due to language.
