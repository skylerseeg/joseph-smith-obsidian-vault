**[2]** This ordination may have occurred in preparation for Boynton to undertake another mission with Zebedee Coltrin. On 19 November, Boynton and Coltrin, accompanied by William Cahoon, left Kirtland, Ohio, on a mission to the East. (Coltrin, Diary and Notebook, 19 Nov. 1832.)
