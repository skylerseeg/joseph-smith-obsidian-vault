## Full Transcript

Joseph said When he was asking to know of the time of Christs second Coming he obtained for answer thy <​my​> son if thou shalt live untill thou art Eighty five years old thou shalt see the son of man on Earth [p. [16]]

  Joseph was born in SharonTownship charter granted, 17 Aug. 1761. Area settled, by 1765. Township organized, 1768. Included village of Sharon. Population of township in 1800 about 1,200; in 1810 about 1,400; and in 1830 about 1,500. Joseph Smith Sr. rented sixty-eight-acre farm from...More Info Windsor County Vermont Dec 23 1805 He is therefore now 37 years old last Dec which leaves 48 years yet to transpire untill the tim[e] of Promise that Joseph should see Christ [p.
