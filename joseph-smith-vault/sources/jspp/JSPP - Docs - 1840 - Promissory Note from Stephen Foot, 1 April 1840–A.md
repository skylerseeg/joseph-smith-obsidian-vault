## Full Transcript

NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info April 1st. 1840 $225. On or before the first day of April one thousand eight hundred and forty four I promise to pay Mr Joseph Smith or order the Sum of Two hundred and twenty five dollars with interest for value received. Signature of Stephen Foot.Stephen Foot [p. [1]]
