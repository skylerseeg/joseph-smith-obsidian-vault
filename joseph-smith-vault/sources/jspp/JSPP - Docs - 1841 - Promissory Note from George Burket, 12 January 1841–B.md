## Full Transcript

City of NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info Jany 12, 1841 $100..— On or before the twelfth day of January one thousand eight hundred and forty three I promise to pay Mr Joseph Smith or order the Sum of one hundred dollars with interest, for value received. Signature of George Burket.George Burket18 Oct. 1788–15 Mar. 1871. Store owner/keeper, carpenter. Born in Bedford, Bedford Co., Pennsylvania. Son of George Burket Sr. and Catharine Swovelin.
