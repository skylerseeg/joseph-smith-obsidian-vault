## Full Transcript

NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info, Ill. November 1st 1839 On or before the first day of November one thousand Eight hundred and fortynine I promise to pay Jose[ph] Smith or order the sum of Three hundred and ninety dollars with intere[s]t from this date. This interest to be paid on the first of November of every year. In witness my [h]and & seal. Signature of Lyman Wight.Lyman Wight9 May 1796–31 Mar. 1858. Farmer.
