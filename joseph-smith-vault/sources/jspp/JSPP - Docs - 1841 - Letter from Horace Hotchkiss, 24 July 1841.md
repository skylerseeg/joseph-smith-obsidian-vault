**[1]** This letter is apparently no longer extant.
**[2]** Galland evidently left for Nauvoo sometime between 24 and 29 July. By 10 October he had still not reached Nauvoo. He evidently returned to the region sometime prior to 10 December 1841, when he settled at Keokuk rather than Nauvoo. (Letter from William Smith, 5 Aug. 1841; Letter from Smith Tuttle, ca. 15 Sept. 1841; Isaac Galland, Keokuk, Iowa Territory, to JS, Nauvoo, IL, 11 Dec. 1841, JS Collection, CHL.)
**[3]** JS owed Hotchkiss, Tuttle, and Gillet $110,000 for lands in Nauvoo that he purchased on behalf of the church in 1839. The annual interest payment of $3,000 was due in August. (Bond from Horace Hotchkiss, 12 Aug. 1839–A.)
**[4]** By this time, Hyrum Smith was likely already on his way back to Nauvoo. He arrived there sometime prior to 12 August 1841. (Letter to Horace Hotchkiss, 25 Aug. 1841; JS History, vol. C-1 Addenda Book, 10–11.)
**[5]** James Ivins and Charles Ivins, church members who lived in New Jersey, gave Hotchkiss a note for $2,500, which was eventually used to pay off the debt JS owed Hotchkiss on the William White purchase. (Letter from William Smith, 5 Aug. 1841; Letter from Smith Tuttle, ca. 15 Sept. 1841; Letter from Horace Hotchkiss, 11 Oct. 1841; Letter from Horace Hotchkiss, 9 Nov. 1841; see also Promissory Note to Horace Hotchkiss, 23 Oct. 1840.)
