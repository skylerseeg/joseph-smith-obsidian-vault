## Full Transcript

There is sold to As[a]hel PerryPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info the following discribed piece or Lot of land situated on this west side of Main <​street​> and directly opposite to and west of Lot No 2 in block 40— in NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info— Said Lot is not plotted in the plat drawn for NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info but Contains 132 rods of land by being twelve rods in length east & west and eleven rods wide north & south And was at the time when bought probably worth about $800"— That however as to price & terms is refered to Br Joseph— Bought about the 15 March 1840— EE— H[enry] G Sherwood20 Apr. 1785–24 Nov. 1867. Surveyor. Born at Kingsbury, Washington Co., New York. Son of Newcomb Sherwood and a woman whose maiden name was Tolman (first name unidentified). Married Polly by 17 May 1823.
