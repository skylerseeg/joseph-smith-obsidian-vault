## Full Transcript

NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info Hancock— County, Ills Augst. 25 1841 Received of E. Evens & Co. pr. Wm. Allen Seventy two Bottles of “Horning’s Vegetable Ague Syrup,” for which I promise to pay, when called for, One Dollar per Bottle, or return the Medicine in good order. Signature of Ephraim Potter on behalf of JS.Jos Smith Per Eph[rai]m. Potter7 June 1817–after Sept. 1845. Born in Monmouth Co., New Jersey. Son of Ephraim Potter and Hannah Woodmansee.
