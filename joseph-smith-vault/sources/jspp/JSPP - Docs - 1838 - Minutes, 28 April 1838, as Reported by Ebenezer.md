**[2]** When cases were brought before the council, the counselors were numbered, and one or more odd-numbered counselors represented the plaintiff, with the same number of even-numbered counselors representing the defendant. The responsibilities for representing the two parties seem to have rotated through the council. In the council meeting of 28 April, it was the duty of counselors 9 and 10—George M.
