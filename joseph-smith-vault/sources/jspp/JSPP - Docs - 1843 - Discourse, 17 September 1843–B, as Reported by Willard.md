## Full Transcript

P.M. Mr Blodget th[e] unitarian min[i]ster p[r]eached was gratifi[e]d with his [p. [98]]

  serm[o]n in gene[r]al— but differd in opini[o]n on some po[i]nts— on which I freely ixpressed myself to his gre[a]t satisfacti[o]n on perscutin [persecution] making the work spread.— by rooting up a fl[o]wer garden or kicking back the sun.1Blodget spoke on religious liberty, taking his text from John 18:38. Following Blodget’s address, JS spoke, “disproving the idea that persecution causes a good work to prosper” and addressing the proper use of medicine—a topic he had spoken on earlier in the year. (Levi Richards, Journal, 17 Sept. 1843; JS, Journal, 13 Apr. 1843.)Comprehensive Works CitedRichards, Levi. Journals, 1840–1853. Levi Richards, Papers, 1837–1867. CHL. MS 1284, box 1.
