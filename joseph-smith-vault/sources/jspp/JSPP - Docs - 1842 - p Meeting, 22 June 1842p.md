## Full Transcript

An Ordinances repealing all ordinances, laws, & resolutions, relative to The changing of the names of streets. Sec 1. Be it ordained by the city council of the city of NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info, that all ordinances, laws, & resolutions, that have been passed by the city council, changing the names of streets, be, & the same are hereby repealed. Sec 2. That the names of all streets in the city of NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info, that were named at the time that the city plot was drawn out; & as the plot stands recorded in the recorder’s office, retain their original names. Sec 3. That those streets that have been named since the plot was recorded, & have not had their names changed, also retain the names <​that were​>Insertion in handwriting of James Sloan. first given to them.
