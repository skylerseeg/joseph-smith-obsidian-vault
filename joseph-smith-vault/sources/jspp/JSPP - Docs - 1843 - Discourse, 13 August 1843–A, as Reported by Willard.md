Some of JS’s religious contemporaries, such as William Miller, George Storrs, and Luther Lee, similarly discussed, and in some cases debated, the concept of annihilation. JS’s early revelations painted a picture of the afterlife that did not easily conform to the traditional heaven and hell binary of other Christian denominations. An 1829 revelation and an 1832 vision, for example, indicated that through Jesus Christ’s atonement nearly all people would inherit one of three degrees of heavenly glory after death.
