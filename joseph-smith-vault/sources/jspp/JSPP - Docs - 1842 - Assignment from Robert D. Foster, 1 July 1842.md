## Full Transcript

Know all men by these presents that for and in consideration of value received by contract with & agreement I Robert. D. Foster14 Mar. 1811–1 Feb. 1878. Justice of the peace, physician, land speculator. Born in Braunston, Northamptonshire, England. Son of John Foster and Jane Knibb. Married Sarah Phinney, 18 July 1837, at Medina Co., Ohio. Baptized into Church of Jesus Christ of ...View Full Bio do this day make over and assign to General Joseph Smith all my right title Interest & Claim to the North West Qr of section nine in township (6) six north and range eight west in Hancock CountyFormed from Pike Co., 1825. Described in 1837 as predominantly prairie and “deficient in timber.” Early settlers came mainly from mid-Atlantic and southern states. Population in 1835 about 3,200; in 1840 about 9,900; and in 1844 at least 15,000. Carthage ...More Info Illinois said land is to be accounted for by said Joseph Smith at the ratio that he sells again 80 acres at twenty one twenty Dollars pr acre and the ballance to be adjusted hereafter when sale is made then the price is to be fixt and the further arrangmt made that is necessary this is however a full conveyance of my right to the premises to all intents and purposes and my paper will be taken at par so far as they are held or taken up by Joseph Smith as from Erie Rhodes or his executors or assigns as witness my hand & seal this first day of July AD 1842 R.
