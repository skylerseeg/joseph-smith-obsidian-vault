## Source Note

In late February or early March 1842, JS produced a rough draft of an editorial regarding the forthcoming publication of the Book of Abraham. Though the editorial is undated, its statements concerning the Book of Abraham and JS’s role in editing material for the paper suggest that JS expected to publish the editorial in the 1 March 1842 issue of the Times and Seasons. JS had recently become the newspaper’s editor.
