The note is representative of the many routine documents produced by JS and his clerks in the daily course of fulfilling his civic responsibilities. The note to Sloan was the result of a legal case, State of Illinois v. Daniel Brown and Thomas Edwards, over which JS had presided as a justice of the peace in the mayor’s court. On 15 November 1842, JS found insufficient evidence against the defendants to sustain the charge that they had stolen some logs, and he discharged Brown and Edwards from custody.
