## Full Transcript

TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN: This certifies that Justus [Justis] Morse—— has been received into the church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints, organized on the sixth of April, in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and thirty, and has been ordained an Elder—— according to the rules and regulations of said church; and is duly authorised to preach the gospel, agreeably to the authority of that office.
