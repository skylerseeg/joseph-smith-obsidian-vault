## Full Transcript

$50.— On demand for value received I promise to pay Abraham HoaglandView Full Bio or bearer the just and full sum of Fifty dollars with use. Signature of JS.Joseph Smith NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri.
