Whitney, JS, Rigdon, and others) were “bound together . . . in your several Stewartships to manage the literary & Mercantile concerns & the Bishoprick both in the Land of Zion & in the Land of Kirtland.” (Revelation, 1 Mar. 1832 [D&C 78:3–4, 9]; Revelation, 26 Apr. 1832 [D&C 82:11–12].)
