## Footnotes

**[1]** Only JS and Hyrum Smith signed this authorization on behalf of the church’s First Presidency. The presidency at this time also included Sidney Rigdon, whose service was intermittent. William Law was removed from the presidency sometime before 8 January 1844, and Amasa Lyman was appointed as a counselor to the First Presidency on 4 February 1843. A January 1841 revelation named Hyrum Smith as church patriarch as well as a “prophet and a seer and a revelator” and granted him the same “keys” that were once held by Oliver Cowdery, who was sustained as “second elder of this Church” at the organization’s founding meeting on 6 April 1830. (Law, Record of Doings, 4 and 8 Jan. 1844, in Cook, William Law, 41–42, 46; JS, Journal, 4 Feb. 1843; Revelation, 19 Jan. 1841 [D&C 124:91–95]; JS History, vol. A-1, 30.)
