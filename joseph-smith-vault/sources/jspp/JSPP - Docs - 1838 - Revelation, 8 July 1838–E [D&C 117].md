However, that plan may have changed with the revelation’s chastisement of Whitney and its statement that he would serve in Adam-ondi-Ahman as a bishop “not in name but in deed.” Bishop Edward Partridge wrote to Whitney about the revelation two weeks later, stating, “I some expect that you will have to take the tithing at Adamondiahman the same as I have to here.” (Minutes, 28 June 1838; Edward Partridge, Far West, MO, to Newel K. Whitney, Kirtland, OH, 24 July 1838, in Reynolds Cahoon, Far West, MO, to Newel K. Whitney, Kirtland, OH, 23 July 1838, CHL.)
