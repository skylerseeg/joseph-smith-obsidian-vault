## Full Transcript

NOTICE. SOMETIME in April or May last, the S. B. [steamboat] Maid of Iowa left with the subscriber a Feather Bed, two Pillows and one Bed Quilt which the owner can have by proving property and paying charges.1In early April 1843, the Maid of Iowa transported Latter-day Saint immigrants from St. Louis to Nauvoo; in May, the vessel began operating as a ferry between Nauvoo and Montrose, Iowa Territory. It is possible that the unknown passenger left the bedding on the boat after one of these voyages.
