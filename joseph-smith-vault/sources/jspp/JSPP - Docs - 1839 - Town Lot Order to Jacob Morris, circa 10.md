## Full Transcript

Blocks) Lot No 3 — is selected by Jacob Morris and Stands valued at $750 91) It is Situated near to where the house of the LordLocated in portion of Nauvoo known as the bluff. JS revelation dated Jan. 1841 commanded Saints to build temple and hotel (Nauvoo House). Cornerstone laid, 6 Apr. 1841. Saints volunteered labor, money, and other resources for temple construction. Construction...More Info will be built— No particular terms of payment is agreed on— leave with Prest Hyrum [Smith]9 Feb. 1800–27 June 1844. Farmer, cooper. Born at Tunbridge, Orange Co., Vermont. Son of Joseph Smith Sr. and Lucy Mack.
