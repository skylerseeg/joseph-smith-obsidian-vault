**[5]** Congress provided for “organizing, arming, and disciplining” militias. States, however, had the constitutional responsibility for prescribing discipline and enforcing compliance. **[6]** Stephen A. Douglas was appointed to the state supreme court in mid-February 1841. He had previously served as the Illinois secretary of state. (See Commission from Thomas Carlin, 10 March 1841.)
