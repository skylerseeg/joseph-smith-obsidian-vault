Saxton, 4 Jan. 1833.)
**[4]** The word “to” is missing at this location in the copy in Lucy Mack Smith’s history. (Lucy Mack Smith, History, 1845, 228.)
**[5]** See Book of Mormon, 1830 ed., 81 [2 Nephi 9:25]; Luke 12:48; and Revelation, 26 Apr. 1832 [D&C 82:3].
**[6]** See Matthew 4:4; and Deuteronomy 8:3. Instead of “the Lord,” the copy in Lucy Mack Smith’s history has “God.” (Lucy Mack Smith, History, 1845, 229.)
