## Full Transcript

In Consideration of Two hundred dollars to Us to be paid as follows viz, One hundred dollars at the expiration of Ten years from the date hereof— with interest at six per cent payable annually— And one hundred dollars at the expiration of Twenty years from the date hereof, with interest at six per cent payable annually— The first payment of interest on both sums to be made one year from the date hereof— We, Joseph Smith Jr, Sidney Rigdon19 Feb. 1793–14 July 1876. Tanner, farmer, minister. Born at St. Clair, Allegheny Co., Pennsylvania. Son of William Rigdon and Nancy Gallaher. Joined United Baptists, ca. 1818. Preached at Warren, Trumbull Co., Ohio, and vicinity, 1819–1821. Married Phebe...View Full Bio, and Hiram [Hyrum] Smith9 Feb. 1800–27 June 1844. Farmer, cooper. Moved to Randolph, Orange Co., 1802; to Tunbridge, before May 1803; to Royalton, Windsor Co., Vermont, 1804; to Sharon, Windsor Co., by...View Full Bio do hereby all of NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri.
