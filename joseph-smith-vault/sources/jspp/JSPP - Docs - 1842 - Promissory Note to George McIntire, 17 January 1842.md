## Full Transcript

$116.90NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info January the 17th. 1842Six months after date I promise to pay or Cause to be paid unto George McIntire or order his heirs or assigns the sum of one hundred and sixteen dollars and Ninty Cents with ten per cent premium, and five per cent Interest per annum it be for Vallue Recd. Witness my hand and seal[Joseph Smith]in presence ofWillard Richards24 June 1804–11 Mar. 1854. Teacher, lecturer, doctor, clerk, printer, editor, postmaster.
