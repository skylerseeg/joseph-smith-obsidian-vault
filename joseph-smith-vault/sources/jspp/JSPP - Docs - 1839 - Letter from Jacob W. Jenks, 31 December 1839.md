**[3]** In accordance with instruction given in a December 1833 revelation, church conferences appointed JS, Rigdon, and Higbee to petition the federal government for redress. (Revelation, 16–17 Dec. 1833 [D&C 101:86–92]; Minutes, 4–5 May 1839; Minutes and Discourses, 5–7 Oct. 1839.)
