NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info June 20, 1844 Brothers Ballentine [Richard Ballantyne]View Full Bio & [Peter] SlaterCa. 1821–before 1870. Miller. Born in Lancashire, England. Son of Peter Slater. Married Sarah Nutter, 16 Feb. 1840, in Burnley, Lancashire. Resident of Whalley, Lancashire, 1841. Baptized into Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, by 1842. Immigrated...View Full Bio, on information from you by J. McIllrick [John McIlwrick]View Full Bio, I would advise that your families remain where they are— and be quiet. as the mob will not be likely to disturb them, but any amount of wheat or provisions you may have, you had better remove to without delay to NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info, as it will be better for you to bring it here and have your pay, than to leave it for the mob to consume, & destroy. I remain your Broth[e]r in Christ Jesus. Signature of JS in the handwriting of William W. Phelps.Joseph Smith BallentineView Full Bio & SlaterCa. 1821–before 1870. Miller.
