## Full Transcript

Joseph spoke about a conspiracy to kill him — he said he would not say anything about the characters of the Laws, [Joseph H.] Jackson?–? Moved to Nauvoo, Hancock Co., Illinois, by fall 1842. Moved to Carthage, Hancock Co., winter 1842–1843. Returned to Nauvoo, spring 1843. Hired to sell land for JS, 20 May 1843.
