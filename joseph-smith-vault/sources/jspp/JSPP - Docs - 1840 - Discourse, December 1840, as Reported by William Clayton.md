## Full Transcript

A key by Joseph Smith Decm 1840—W. C. [William Clayton]17 July 1814–4 Dec. 1879. Bookkeeper, clerk. Born at Charnock Moss, Penwortham, Lancashire, England. Son of Thomas Clayton and Ann Critchley. Married Ruth Moon, 9 Oct. 1836, at Penwortham. Baptized into Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints by Heber...View Full Bio If an Angel or spirit appears offer him your hand; if he is a spirit from God he will stand still and not offer you his hand. If from the Devil he will either shrink back from you or offer his hand, which if he does you will feel nothing, but be deceived. A good Spirit will not deceive. Angels are beings who have bodies and appear to men in the form of man.1Discussing the biblical figure Abel, JS told the general conference of the church in Nauvoo on 5 October 1840 that he “died a righteous man, and therefore has become . . . an angel of God by receiving his body from the dead.” (Instruction on Priesthood, ca. 5 Oct. 1840.) The only known account is the one featured here, inscribed by L. John Nuttall in the opening pages of his 1880 notebook.
