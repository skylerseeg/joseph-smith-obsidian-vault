## Full Transcript

Zera[h] S. Cole20 Apr. 1805–14 Feb. 1886. Farmer, tanner. Born in Middlebury, Addison Co., Vermont. Son of John Cole and Cynthia Smith. Moved to Whitesboro, Oneida Co., New York, by Nov. 1831. Married Lydia Ann Childs. Baptized into Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day ...View Full Bio Blessing. The Lord called you when you obeyed the Gospel and you are one of the Seventy. and he will receive nothing but all your time and talents. You are disposed to be a son of Thunder,1See Mark 3:17. but if you are not very faithful you will fall and another will take your crown. But if you are faithful, you shall over come all things,2See Revelation, 25 Jan. 1832–A [D&C 75:16, 22]. and if so in your old age this pleasing reflection shall be your greatest joy. You shall pass through a scene where you shall hear many cry Hosanna, Hosanna. because they heard the truth at your mouth. <​Am[e]n​> [p. 171]
