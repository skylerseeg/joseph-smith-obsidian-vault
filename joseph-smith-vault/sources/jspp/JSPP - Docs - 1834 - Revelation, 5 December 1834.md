## Full Transcript

Verily, condemnation resteth upon you, who are appointed to lead my Chu[r]ch, and to be saviors of men:1See Revelation, 24 Feb. 1834 [D&C 103:9]. and also upon the church: And there must needs be a repentance and a refor[m]ation among you, in all things, in your ensamples before the Chuch, and before the world, in all your manners, habits and customs, and salutations one toward another—rendering unto every man the respect due the office, and calling, and priesthood, whereunto I the Lord have appointed and ordained you. Amen. [p. 17]
