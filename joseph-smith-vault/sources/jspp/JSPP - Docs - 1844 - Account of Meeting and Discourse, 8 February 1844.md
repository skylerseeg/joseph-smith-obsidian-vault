## Footnotes

**[1]** The Saints were driven out of Missouri in winter 1838–1839. JS and other church leaders made numerous appeals to the governments of Missouri, Illinois, and the United States for reparations for lost property as well as the protection of the Saints’ civil rights. (See, for example, Benjamin Kendrick et al., De Witt, MO, to Lilburn W. Boggs, Petition, 22 Sept. 1838, copy, Mormon War Papers, Missouri State Archives, Jefferson City; Memorial to the United States Senate and House of Representatives, ca. 30 Oct. 1839–27 Jan. 1840; and JS et al., Memorial to U.S. Senate and House of Representatives, 28 Nov. 1843, Record Group 46, Records of the U.S. Senate, National Archives, Washington DC.)
