## Full Transcript

Know all men by these presents that I Jahial [Jahiel] Savage of the County of HancockFormed from Pike Co., 1825. Described in 1837 as predominantly prairie and “deficient in timber.” Early settlers came mainly from mid-Atlantic and southern states. Population in 1835 about 3,200; in 1840 about 9,900; and in 1844 at least 15,000. Carthage ...More Info and State of IllinoisBecame part of Northwest Territory of U.S., 1787. Admitted as state, 1818. Population in 1840 about 480,000. Population in 1845 about 660,000. Plentiful, inexpensive land attracted settlers from northern and southern states. Following expulsion from Missouri...More Info am held and firmly bound to Joseph Smith, Trustee in trust for the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints, in the sum of One thousand dollars for the payment of which well and truly to be made I bind myself, my heirs executors and administrators firmly by these presents—: Now the condition of the above obligation is such that whereas the said Jahiel Savage has been appointed by the Church of Jesus Christ to collect funds for the building of the TempleLocated in portion of Nauvoo known as the bluff. JS revelation dated Jan. 1841 commanded Saints to build temple and hotel (Nauvoo House). Cornerstone laid, 6 Apr. 1841. Saints volunteered labor, money, and other resources for temple construction. Construction...More Info in the City of NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info, Now if the said Jahiel Savage shall well and truly perform the said duties and make just and true returns to the said Joseph Smith Trustee as aforesaid of all monies or properties he may collect, and shall pay over <​the same​> to the said Joseph Smith trustee as aforesaid without delay, then this bond to become null and void, other wise to be and remain in full force and vertu Given under my hand & seal this 15th. day of May A. D. 1841 Signature of Jahiel Savage.Jahiel Savage LS [p. [1]]

  Docket in handwriting of Robert B. Thompson.Jahiel Savage Bond
