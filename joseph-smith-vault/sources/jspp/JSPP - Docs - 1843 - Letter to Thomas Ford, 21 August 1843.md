This was technically a violation of English common law, which forbade courts from looking behind the indictment or arrest warrant to inquire into the evidence leading to the arrest in habeas corpus hearings. Illinois’s statute governing habeas corpus also forbade courts from inquiring “into the legality or justice of a judgment or decree of a court legally constituted” beyond accusations of fraud or bribery.
