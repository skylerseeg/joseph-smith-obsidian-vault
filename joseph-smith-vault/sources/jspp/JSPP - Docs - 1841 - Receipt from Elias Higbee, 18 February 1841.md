## Full Transcript

Received of Joseph Smith Trustee in trust for the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter day Saints 2 dollars to be appropriated to the use of the house of the LordLocated in portion of Nauvoo known as the bluff. JS revelation dated Jan. 1841 commanded Saints to build temple and hotel (Nauvoo House). Cornerstone laid, 6 Apr. 1841. Saints volunteered labor, money, and other resources for temple construction.
