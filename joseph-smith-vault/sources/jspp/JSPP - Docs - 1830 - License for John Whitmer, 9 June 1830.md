**[3]** Articles and Covenants, ca. Apr. 1830 [D&C 20:37].
**[4]** TEXT: A thick black ink smudge is found below Oliver Cowdery’s signature, running about the length of his name. Cowdery presumably made the smudge to ensure that no other signatures were added below his.
**[5]** The terms “first Elder” and “second Elder” were ecclesiastical titles. (See, for example, JS History, vol. A-1, 18.)
