## Full Transcript

$1415.00 Nine months after date for full value received we or either of us promise to pay Hugh Rhodes as administrator for Erie Rhodes deceased the just and full sum of fourteen hundred and fifteen dollars with interest at the rate of six per cent per annum from maturity untill paid. Witness our hands and seals this 12th. day of October A. D. 1843 Signatures of William Clayton and JS.William Clayton17 July 1814–4 Dec. 1879. Bookkeeper, clerk.
