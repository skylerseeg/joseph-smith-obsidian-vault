## Full Transcript

preached on the subject of the spirit of <​Elias​> Elijah. Elias & Mesiah clearly defini[n]g the offices of the 3 personages. The Savior will not come this year. <​nor 40 year to come.​> The bow has been seen in the cloud & in that year that the bow is seen seed time and harvest will be. but when the bow ceases to be seen look out for a famine.
