Twelve Dollars, being the Sum allowed him by the City Council, upon the 22nd day of January last, for the use of the Room & Fuel & Candles for twelve Days. This order to be Signed by the Mayor before Presentation for Payment.— June 11th. 1842. Signature of JS.Joseph Smith Mayor James Sloan28 Oct. 1792–24 Oct. 1886.
