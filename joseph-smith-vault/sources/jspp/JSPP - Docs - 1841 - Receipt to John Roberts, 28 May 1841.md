NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info. May 28th 1841 Received of John RobertsView Full Bio the following goods on the WigginsView Full Bio farm, to wit: Amt. goods put in by C. S. Harris, as pr. the deduction on his Bill. . . . 25.oo Cash—— 25.00 $50.oo Joseph Smith pr. Jno. S. Fullmer21 July 1807–8 Oct. 1883. Farmer, newsman, postmaster, teacher, merchant. Born at Huntington, Luzerne Co., Pennsylvania. Son of Peter Fullmer and Susannah Zerfass. Moved to Nashville, Davidson Co., Tennessee, spring 1832.
