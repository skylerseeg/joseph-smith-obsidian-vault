## Footnotes

**[1]** The document is dated 1 July 1839, but this is a scribal error. Land was not being sold in Nauvoo until the fall of 1839, and the preprinted form Sherwood inscribed was in use from November 1839 through at least September 1840. It is likely that Sherwood mistook either the month or the year when he filled out the form.
