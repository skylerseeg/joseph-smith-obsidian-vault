There is sold to Lyman StoddardView Full Bio the following discribed Lot— piece or parcel of land Situated in the plot of CommerceLocated near middle of western boundary of state, bordering Mississippi River. European Americans settled area, 1820s. From bank of river, several feet above high-water mark, ground described as nearly level for six or seven blocks before gradually sloping...More Info laying on Main Street that runs N & S— through NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri.
