**[1]** Harriet Adams was born in Goshen, New York, on 31 January 1787 to Harry and Elizabeth Denton. She married James Adams in about 1809 in New York and bore five children, four of whom lived past infancy: Lovenia, Charlotte, Lucien, and Vienna. She appears to have been baptized into the church before 5 October 1841, when she received a patriarchal blessing.
