## Footnotes

**[1]** Ripley presented a claim to the Nauvoo City Council on 1 June 1843, which was allowed and this order written. The transaction was recorded in the Nauvoo City Treasury Ledger on 12 July 1843, though the order is misdated in the ledger to 10 June 1843. (Claim from Alanson Ripley, 17 May 1843; Nauvoo City Council Rough Minute Book, Nov. 1842–Jan. 1844, 18; Nauvoo City Treasury Ledger, 48.)
