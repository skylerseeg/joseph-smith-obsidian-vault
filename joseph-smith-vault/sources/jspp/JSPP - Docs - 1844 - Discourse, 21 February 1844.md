## Full Transcript

Among other things said that men may preach & practice evry thing except those things which God commands us to do & will be damned at last we may tithe rue annis [anise] & cummin [cumin] & still not obey the commandments of God1See Matthew 23:23. the object with me is to obey & teach others to obey God in just what he tells us to do It mattereth not whether the principle is popular or unpopular I will always maintain a true principl even if I stand alone in it [p. [195]]
