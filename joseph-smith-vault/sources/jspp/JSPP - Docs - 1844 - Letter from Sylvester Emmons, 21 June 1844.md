## Full Transcript

To the Hon members of the City Council of NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info Sirs I see by a report of Council I am suspended untill as a member of the Council untill further investigation to avoid that necessity I herewith tender to you a resignation of all the rights and immunities of said membership the fine that was imposed upon me during my absence through the fall, winter & Spring I respectfully ask to have remitted the Code of criminal Laws which I drew up for the action of the Council when respectfully addressed upon the subject they shall be at the service of the Council Yours Respectfully Sylvester Emmons28 Feb. 1808–15 Nov. 1881. Lawyer, newspaper editor/publisher. Born in Readington Township, Hunterdon Co., New Jersey. Son of Abraham Emmons and Margaret Vlerebome. Moved to Philadelphia, 1831. Moved to Illinois, 1840.
