## Full Transcript

R[oger] OrtonCa. 1799–1851. Miller. Son of Roger Orton and Esther Avery. Moved to Geneseo, Ontario Co., New York, by 1810. Married Clarissa Bicknell, ca. 1822. Baptized into Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Ordained an elder, by 1834. Participated in Camp ...View Full Bio’s Blessing You shall be blessed more than once your heart desired. You shall go forth and preach the everlasting gospel. You shall be delivered from your enemies. If there be no other way to escape them, you shall walk upon the waters. We ordain you to be one of the Seventy. Your tongue shall be loosed,1See Revelation, May 1829–A [D&C 11:21]; Revelation, Apr. 1830–B [D&C 23:3]; and Revelation, Sept. 1830–F [D&C 31:3]. The Heavens shall be open [upon?] your head, And you shall say as Paul of old, I have heard things unutterable,2See 2 Corinthians 12:4. [p. 165]

  You shall have the gift of faith. You shall have power to heal the sick, open the eyes of the blind; loose the tongue of the dumb and cause the lame to leap as an heart [hart].3See Isaiah 35:6. You shall escape destruction, and bring to pass much righteousness [p. 166]
