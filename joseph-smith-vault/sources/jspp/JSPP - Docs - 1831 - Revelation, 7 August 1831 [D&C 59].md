It is not, however, in the copy made by John Whitmer in Revelation Book 1, nor is it included in the version published in the July 1832 issue of The Evening and the Morning Star. (Hyde and Smith, Notebook, [74]; Revelation Book 1, pp. 98–100; “Commandment for Keeping the Sabbath, &c.,” The Evening and the Morning Star, July 1832, [1].)
