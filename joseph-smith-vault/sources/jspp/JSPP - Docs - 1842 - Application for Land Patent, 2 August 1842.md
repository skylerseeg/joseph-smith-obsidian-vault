Described in 1837 as predominantly prairie and “deficient in timber.” Early settlers came mainly from mid-Atlantic and southern states. Population in 1835 about 3,200; in 1840 about 9,900; and in 1844 at least 15,000. Carthage ...More Info Ill County, and State of IllinoisBecame part of Northwest Territory of U.S., 1787.
