Though he began a draft of the proclamation, it was not complete by the time of his death in August 1841. In November 1843, JS renewed the instruction, directing Willard Richards, Orson Hyde, John Taylor, and William W. Phelps to write a proclamation “to the Kings &c of the earth.” The proclamation was not published prior to JS’s death and was eventually written by Parley P. Pratt and published in 1845 as Proclamation of the Twelve Apostles of the Church of Jesus Christ, of Latter-day Saints.
