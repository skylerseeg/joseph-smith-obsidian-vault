## Full Transcript

February 15th 1841 This is to certify that I have received of Joseph Smith the folowing property for my use namely thre cows one horse two waggons one croscut saw one clock three beds and bedding one stove and cooking utensials one carpet a quantity of carpenter and joiner tools and the lot on which I live and two saddles one one horse harness. the above named articles I <​am​> to use till called for by the afore said Joseph Smith Signature of Jabez Durfee.Jabis Durfee23 Dec. 1791–Apr. 1867.
