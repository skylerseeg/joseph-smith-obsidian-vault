**[3]** Latter-day Saint Elizabeth Haven wrote in late February 1839 that the people of Quincy donated between $400 and $500 to assist church members, perhaps in cash and other contributions. “God has opened their hearts to receive us,” she noted. “We are hungry and they feed us, naked and clothe us.” The Quincy Democratic Association held a number of meetings in February 1839 resolving to help the refugee Mormons find employment, shelter, and supplies. Other family members had more success in sending letters with Rogers. (Letter from Don Carlos Smith and William Smith, 6 Mar. 1839; Mary Fielding Smith, [Quincy, IL], to Hyrum Smith, 11 Apr. 1839, Mary Fielding Smith, Collection, CHL; see also Letter from Emma Smith, 7 Mar. 1839.)
