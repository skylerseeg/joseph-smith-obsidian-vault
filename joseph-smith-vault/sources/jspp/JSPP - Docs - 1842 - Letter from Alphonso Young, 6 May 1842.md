(Times and Seasons, 15 June 1842; Articles and Covenants, ca. Apr. 1830 [D&C 20:38–43, 64]; Resolution, ca. 8 Apr. 1838; Far West and Nauvoo Elders’ Certificates, 113.)

**[6]** Young appears to have been acquainted with Frost. **[7]** Smoot was preaching in and around Smith County during the summer of 1840 and probably met Young during that time. At a conference held in Nauvoo in August 1841, Smoot was appointed to preach in Charleston, South Carolina. While making his way to Charleston that fall, he returned to Smith County and became reacquainted with Young, with whom he stayed during part of the fall and winter. (Abraham Smoot, Whitleyville, TN, Letter to the Editor, Times and Seasons, Oct. 1840, 1:181; Minutes, 16 Aug. 1841; Smoot, Diary, 25 Aug. 1841; 24–25 Sept. 1841; 12 Dec. 1841; 18 Jan. 1842.)
