Moved to Nashville, Davidson Co., Tennessee, spring 1832. Surveyor, lawyer. Born at New York. Son of Asa Ripley and Polly Deforest. Married Sarah Finkle. Resided in Massachusetts, 1827. Member of Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in Ohio. (Claim from Alanson Ripley, 18 May 1842; Nauvoo City Council Rough Minute Book, Jan.–Nov. 1842, 29; Nauvoo City Treasury Ledger, 2, 48.) or Order, Twenty four Dollars, which was this day allowed him by the City Council, for surveying the Burying Grounds, this order to be signed by the Mayor before presentation for payment.
