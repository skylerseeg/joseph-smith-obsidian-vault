**[2]** Although the legion mobilized on 9 December 1843, it apparently received no specific orders until 18 December 1843. (Wilson Law to Charles C. Rich, Order, Nauvoo, IL, 9 Dec. 1843, Charles C. Rich, Collection, CHL; Military Order to Wilson Law, 18 Dec. 1843–A; Military Order to Wilson Law, 18 Dec. 1843–B.)
