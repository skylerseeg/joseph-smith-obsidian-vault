## Footnotes

**[1]** In the Old Testament, a solemn assembly was a “holy convocation” of individuals. For Latter-day Saints in 1835, the term had become closely associated with the House of the Lord at Kirtland. The House of the Lord was dedicated on 27 March 1836, and on 30 March a group of three hundred men met there to participate in a solemn assembly. (Leviticus 23:36; Joel 1:14; Revelation, 27–28 Dec. 1832 [D&C 88:70, 117–120]; JS, Journal, 27 and 30 Mar. 1836.)
**[2]** During the 30 March 1836 solemn assembly, Sherman and the other members of the Seventy were instructed to “go to Zion if they please or go wheresoever they will and preach the gospel and let the redemtion of Zion be our object.” (Minutes, 30 Mar. 1836.)
