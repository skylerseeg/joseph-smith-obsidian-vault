**[1]** Parley P. Pratt confirmed that the “oficers and troops, of Jackson County; have Behaved with that Respect, honor and kindness towards us.” (P. Pratt to M. Pratt, 4 Nov. 1838.)

In the wake of the capitulation, other perceived traitors in the war were condemned as being “worse than Hull.” (Taylor, Civil War of 1812, 154–173, 196.)
**[6]** Previously a trusted church leader in Missouri, Corrill became disaffected in summer 1838.
