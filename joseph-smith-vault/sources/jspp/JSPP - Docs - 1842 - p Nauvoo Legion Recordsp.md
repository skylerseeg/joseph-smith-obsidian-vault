Each Company shall be officered with a Captain, three Lieutenants, five Sergeants, one Pioneer, and four Corporals. Sec. 13. The Lieutenant General, and the Major General, may by their joint act, grant brevet commissions to such persons as may merit appointment and promotion at their hands. Sec. 14.
