## Full Transcript

NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info Decr 9th 1842 To the city council of the City of NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info— This may certify that the City Surveyer has Surveyed Back Street from Mulholland Street to Munson Street according to resolution passed January 22nd 1842 Surveyer fees $.1.00) Chainmens fees $.1.00) Also— This may certify that the city surveyer has Surveyed Bluff Street from Mulholland Street to munson Street according to resolution pased July 17th 1842 Surveyers fees $.1.00) chainmens fees $.1.00) Also this cert[if]y that the city Surveyer has Surveyed the Street East of Warsaw Street from parley Street South to to city limits according resolution passed oct 30th 1841 <​See tops of pages 26 & 40.—​>Insertion in handwriting of James Sloan. Surveyers fees $.2.00 Chainmens fees. 2.00 This may certify that the city Surveyer has Servyeed the Street running South from Parly St to lumber St Between Charles Hubbart Bentley and Taylor &c according to Resolution passed March 5th 1842 Surveyers fees $100 Chainmens fees 100 A[lanson] Ripley8 Jan. 1798–before 1860. Surveyor, lawyer. Born at New York. Son of Asa Ripley and Polly Deforest. Married Sarah Finkle. Resided in Massachusetts, 1827. Member of Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in Ohio. Participated in Camp of Israel expedition...View Full Bio} City Surv <​referred to Committee on Claims.—​>Endorsement in handwriting of James Sloan. [p. [1]]
