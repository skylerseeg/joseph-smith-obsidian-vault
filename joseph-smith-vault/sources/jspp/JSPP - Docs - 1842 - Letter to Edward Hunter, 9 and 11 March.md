**[3]** Hunter requested that JS purchase ninety acres of woodland outside of Nauvoo from Chauncey Robison and an additional forty acres from Hugh McFall. He also asked him to contact Jacob Weiler, whom he had hired to build his home in Nauvoo. Hunter also mentioned that he had asked Weiler to arrange for Stephen Winchester or someone else to plow eighty acres of his land and William Garner to fence the property. (Letter from Edward Hunter, 10 Feb. 1842; Jacob Weiler, Nauvoo, IL, to Edward Hunter, Chester Co., PA, 27 Feb. 1842, Edward Hunter, Collection, 1816–1884, CHL.)
**[5]** A January 1841 revelation commanded the Saints to build the Nauvoo temple as well as the Nauvoo House, a boardinghouse that would also serve as a home for JS and his family. (Revelation, 19 Jan. 1841 [D&C 124:55–56].)
**[6]** On 10 February 1842 Hunter wrote JS expressing his intention to donate $800 in support of both construction projects. Hunter had previously shipped goods from Pennsylvania to Nauvoo. (Letter from Edward Hunter, 10 Feb. 1842; Letter from Edward Hunter, 27 Oct. 1841.)
