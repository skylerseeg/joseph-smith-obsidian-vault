Thence South to the southwest corner of said Societies lot, thence east to the centre of the road before mentioned thence Southwardly to the place of beginning. Containing one acre & one hundred and fifty four and half rods.” A copy of this amended deed was made by Orson Hyde from the version in the Geauga County Deed Record Book 24.
