**[1]** Phelps had received a letter from Sally the day before. According to Phelps, when JS read Sally’s letter, he “remarked that it was as easy to shed tears while reading that letter as it was when reading the History of Joseph in Egypt.” (Phelps, “Letters of Faith from Kirtland,” 529.)

Phelps, 27 Nov. 1832; “Some Early Letters of William W. Phelps,” Utah Genealogical and Historical Magazine, Jan. 1940, 29.)
**[4]** Oliver Cowdery told Sally Phelps in August 1835 that she could expect her husband’s return in the spring of 1836 “if all be well.” Phelps actually returned to Missouri by June 1836. (“Some Early Letters of William W. Phelps,” Utah Genealogical and Historical Magazine, Jan. 1940, 29; “2d Series—Letter No. I,” LDS Messenger and Advocate, July 1836, 2:340.)

Phelps, Kirtland, OH, to Sally Waterman Phelps, Liberty, MO, 26 May 1835, William W. Phelps, Papers, BYU.)
