## Full Transcript

J[oseph] B. Noble14 Jan. 1810–17 Aug. 1900. Farmer, miller, stock raiser. Born in Egremont, Berkshire Co., Massachusetts. Son of Ezekiel Noble and Theodotia Bates. Moved to Penfield, Monroe Co., New York, 1815. Moved to Bloomfield, Ontario Co., New York, ca. 1828. Baptized...View Full Bio’s Blessing You are a chosen vessel,1See Acts 9:15; and Book of Mormon, 1830 ed., 579 [Moroni 7:31]. even one of the horns of Joseph. Great things will the Lord require at your hand. for you shall push the righteous together from the ends of the earth, in hundreds and thousands.2See Deuteronomy 33:17. The visions of Heaven shall be opened upon your head. You shall have power over all things in the name of the Lord. Angels saw thee ages since & rejoiced over thee,3See Revelation, 13 Aug. 1831 [D&C 62:3]; and Revelation, 27–28 Dec. 1832 [D&C 88:2]. Amen. [p. 170]
