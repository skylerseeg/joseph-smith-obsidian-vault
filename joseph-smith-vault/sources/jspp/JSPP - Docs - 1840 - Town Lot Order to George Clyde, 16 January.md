Clide [Clyde]View Full Bio at $400;— It is agreed to <​be​> in exchange for Lot No 1 in Block 301See Town Lot Order to George Clyde, 15 Jan. 1840. as he agrees to Make payment of a heavy pair of heavy oxen in the Spring.
