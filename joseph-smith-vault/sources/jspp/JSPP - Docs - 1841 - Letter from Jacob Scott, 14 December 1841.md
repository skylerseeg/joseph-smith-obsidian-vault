## Full Transcript

Decr. 14th, 1841 Brother Joseph. Long continued sickness prevented me from seeing you, which I have greatly desired— I have more reasons than one for this, One principal one is, I want to be more fully reconciled and give that satisfaction which it is my duty to do. I am unable to go to you; I entreat you Brother Joseph give me the opportunity, by coming to see me, before me before I die.
