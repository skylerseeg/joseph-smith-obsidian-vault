## Footnotes

**[1]** JS’s later history states that he and Rigdon left Kirtland on 12 January 1838 at “about 10 o’clock” at night and arrived “at 8 o clock of the morning of the 13th . . . in Norton Township, Medina County, Ohio.” (JS History, vol. B-1, 780.)
**[2]** After receiving word that JS and Rigdon were on their way, the Zion high council in Far West planned to send men with two wagons and $300 to meet the travelers and help them finish their journey.
