## Full Transcript

NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info, September 16th 1841 On or before the first day of May next I promise to pay Erie Rhodes or order the sum of five hundred and thirty five dollars. Value Rece[ive]d. Witness my hand and Seal— [signature torn off] [Joseph Smith] [p. [1]]

  Notation in handwriting of John S. Fullmer.◊◊◊◊◊ved on the [2 words illegible]— thirty five ◊◊◊◊◊◊s— Sept. 27— 1841 Signature of Erie Rhodes.Erie Rhoodes
