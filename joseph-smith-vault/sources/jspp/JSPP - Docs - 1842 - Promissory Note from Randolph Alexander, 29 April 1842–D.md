## Full Transcript

$50 NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info April 29th. 1842 On or before the first day of April one thousand eight hundred and forty six for value received I promise to pay to Joseph Smith as Sole Trustee in Trust &c or bearer the sum of fifty dollars with interest at six pr cent pr annum.—— Randolph AlexanderView Full Bio [p.
