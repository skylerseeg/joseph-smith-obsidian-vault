Now, if said John Steed, his—— heirs, executors, or administrators shall well and truly pay or cause to be paid, the amount of said note with such interest as may accrue thereon, according to the tenor and effect thereof, when due, I the said Joseph Smith as Sole Trustee in Trust etc. bind myself my successors in office, heirs, executors administrators and assigns, to make, or cause to be made, to the said John Steed—— his heirs and assigns, a good and sufficient Deed for the above described lot on or before the first day of January A.
