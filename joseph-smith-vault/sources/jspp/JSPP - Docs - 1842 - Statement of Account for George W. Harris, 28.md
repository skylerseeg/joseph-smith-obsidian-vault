## Full Transcript

Feb 28th 1842Joseph Smith to Geo. W. Harris Drto repairing and Cleaning repeater watch$5.00to repairing and Cleaning lever watch1.75to repairing lapier watch————37½ [p. [1]]

  Feby 28th 1842Recd on the within bill two linning sheats at $2.50 each——$5.00by 2½ yd of janes at 80 Ct per yd—2.00by one small handkerchief at 12 Ct.—12½ $7.12½ [p. [2]]
