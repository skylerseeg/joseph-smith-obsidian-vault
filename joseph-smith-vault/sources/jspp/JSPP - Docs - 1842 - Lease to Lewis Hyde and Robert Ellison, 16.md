## Full Transcript

For and in consideration of the sum of one hundred & fifty dollars, to be paid <​unto the Signer of this instrument​> in five equal, annual payments. from the date hereof by Severally & Jointly by Lewis Hyde & Robert Ellison I Promise & Lease unto the said Lewis Hyde & Robert Ellison a certain Lot of land in the city of NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info known as Lot No one in Block eleven on the Plat of said cityPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info, to the <​use an​> benefit & use of said Hyde & Ellison for the purpose of Erecting & using a lumber & Grail Grain mill, for the term of five years from the date hereof, but should the said Hyde & Ellison fail, for the space of six months next ensueing, to erect & bring into successful operation said grain & Lumber mill, on or near said Lot as aforesaid, then this lease is to be null & void. In witness whereof I have hereunto set my hand at NauvooPrincipal gathering place for Saints following expulsion from Missouri. Beginning in 1839, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased lands in earlier settlement of Commerce and planned settlement of Commerce City, as well as surrounding areas....More Info this 16th day of April A. D. 1842. [p. [1]]

  Docket in handwriting of Willard Richards.Copy of a Lease from J. Smith Lot 1 Bk 11.— to L. Hyde. R. Ellison
