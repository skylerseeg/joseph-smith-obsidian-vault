## Thesis

Joseph Smith's social network was a "dynamic and volatile ecosystem that mirrored the stresses of the American frontier." To understand Smith, one must map the diverse cohorts surrounding him: family, scribes, business partners, and high-level politicians.

---

## Core Argument Structure

### Phase 1 — New York & Pennsylvania (1816–1831)
- Network built on kinship and folk magic subculture.
- Key causal link: Knights & Stowells had surplus resources (horses, grain, paper) to sustain translation.
- Hostility from Chase/Stafford families = broken "money-digging social contract."

### Phase 2 — Ohio / Kirtland (1831–1838)
- Network professionalized. Physicians, lawyers, merchants entered.
- Land bubble: $7 → $44/acre. Kirtland Safety Society failure transformed loyal apostles into internal dissenters.
- Key insight: Smith's leadership increasingly seen as "unilateral in temporal affairs."

### Phase 3 — Missouri Conflict (1838–1839)
- Network became paramilitary. Existential threat from state.
- Betrayal by George M. Hinkle and Lilburn Boggs → Smith develops "theodemocracy" worldview.
- Second-order: radicalization of Mormon leadership → Nauvoo Legion + Council of Fifty.

### Phase 4 — Nauvoo (1839–1844)
- Most complex ecosystem. City government, secret Council of Fifty, plural marriage inner circle.
- Key tension: *Public* network (city council) vs. *Private* network (polygamy/theocracy).
- When insiders like William Law were excluded from private network → catastrophic explosion (*Nauvoo Expositor*).

### Phase 5 — National Stage (1839–1844)
- Presidential campaign = "theological protest" exposing failure of states' rights to protect religious minorities.
- Stephen A. Douglas trajectory: ally for Mormon vote → turned against Smith when politically costly.

## Synthesis Conclusions

1. **Fragility of Inner Circle** — High-velocity turnover among closest associates (Cowdery, Rigdon, Law, Parrish). Proximity to "prophetic power" carried high cognitive/social burden.
2. **Transition to Political Stakeholder** — Network moved from local folklore → national politics. Council of Fifty = "shadow cabinet" for international diplomacy.
3. **Secular Mirror of Journalists** — Quincy, Flagg, Nye White identified Smith as "rare human being" even while labeling doctrine "monstrous."
4. **Betrayal as Legal Mechanism** — Hinkle (Missouri), *Nauvoo Expositor* (Illinois): *insiders* transformed Smith from religious leader to legal defendant.
